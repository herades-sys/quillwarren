Layout grid

4 px base, 8 px rhythm. Spacing runs 3 · 6 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48. That is Nocturne's 0.7× density, rounded to whole pixels.

Desktop editor: top bar 48 · tool rail 52 · canvas fluid (min 560) · inspector 288 · Claude 344. Floating canvas controls sit 12 px in from the canvas edge and never cover more than a third of it.

Controls: 28 / 32 / 36 px on desktop. Tool buttons are 38 × 36. Every touch target is at least 44 × 44.

Phone: status 46 + top bar 52 · map full bleed · toolbar 52 + 28 safe area · sheets rise from the toolbar with an 18 px radius and stop 196 px from the top, so the map always stays visible.
