Ink, stone & parchment

A quiet toolkit for the cartographer's desk. The map is always the hero, so the chrome stays low-chroma stone in both themes. One violet ink accent marks focus, selection and the active tool. Red, amber and blue only appear for status and the three GM markers.

The map leadsThe canvas is parchment in both themes. Panels never float more than a third over it, and nothing around it is saturated.
Keys first, thumbs welcomeEvery GM tool shows its letter. On phones every target is at least 44 px, and sheets replace panels.
Lines, not floodsFrom Nocturne: outlined primary buttons, 8 px radii, a 2 px focus ring, and elevation made of an edge plus soft shade.
