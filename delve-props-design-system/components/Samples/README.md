# Samples

The first batch — four props that set the look for the whole library, shown at map scale (70 px per square) and at 2× with their manifest entries.

Nothing to pass in: the card draws the files in `assets/samples/` as saved. Use it to approve or reject the look before a batch goes on; judge the 70 px column first.

- **table-round** (2×2, `movement`): paper top of uneven north–south boards with sparse grain; two chairs tucked under it, seats in paper with boards parallel to the back, back rails in `shade` (darker turned wood). The chairs face the table and sit a few degrees off-square.
- **chest** (1×1, `movement`): paper lid of three east–west boards; iron bands, hinge straps, handles and lock plate in `shade` at `stroke-detail`; keyhole and the gaps behind the grips in `ink`; lock on the south side.
- **barrel** (1×1, `movement`): two hoops in `shade` split by a texture seam, a paper ring of stave ends with joints, a paper head of three boards, an `ink` bung hole.
- **statue** (1×1, `sight`): bevelled plinth — lower step `shade`, top `paper`; a cloaked figure facing south with its hands on a planted sword, cloak and hair in `shade`, arms and hands in `paper`.

Do: copy these proportions and value patterns for new props of the same kind. Don't: add knots, dots-in-rings or other small glyph-like marks; they read as letters at 70 px.
